# Ya Hala

Ya Hala presents Arab-American field stories and community visits from across the United States.

## Language

**Story**:
One Ya Hala YouTube video presented to a visitor.
_Avoid_: Item, entry

**Story Browser**:
The filterable catalog of Ya Hala stories available at `/stories`.
_Avoid_: Video Browser, video drawer

**About Page**:
The dedicated introduction to Haithem Alqahaf as the host and storyteller of Ya Hala, written primarily for viewers and secondarily for potential partners.
_Avoid_: Bio page, value page
