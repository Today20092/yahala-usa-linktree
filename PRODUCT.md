# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Viewers and people discovering Ya Hala, its host, show, and stories. Businesses and potential sponsors also use the site to understand the work, assess its results, and contact the host.

## Product Purpose

Ya Hala's portfolio and link hub presents Arab American field stories and community visits across the United States. It helps visitors see the work, get updates, and understand the show's reach and results.

The usual visitor journey is to watch, follow, and then contact. Visitors who already know the show need a direct path to contact about sponsorship or collaboration.

Business contact uses Ya Hala's existing Instagram destination.

## Positioning

The work speaks for itself: real stories, community visits, videos, and results demonstrate what Ya Hala does to viewers and prospective sponsors.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve story discovery, social links, published audience statistics, and contact paths.
- The existing site uses Astro, Tailwind, and shadcn components and color tokens. Site content is configured in `src/data/site.yaml`.
- Cloudflare hosts the site. Development deployments use the shared preview Worker specified in `AGENTS.md`; production deployment requires an explicit request.
- Arabic and English content already appear in the site, including video search.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the Ya Hala name, existing host identity, logos, factual content, and community storytelling mission. New claims, sponsorship results, or testimonials require evidence.

## Evidence on Hand

The site configuration contains the host's background and mission, social profiles, featured episodes, and visited places. The repository contains a story catalog and audience statistics. Show those records faithfully; do not fabricate performance or endorsements.

## Product Principles

- Let visitors see the work before asking them to evaluate a pitch.
- Prioritize watching and following while keeping contact easy to find.
- Serve viewers and prospective sponsors on the same portfolio.
- Make reach and results understandable and grounded in available evidence.
