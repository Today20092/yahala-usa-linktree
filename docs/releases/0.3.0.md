# Ya Hala 0.3.0

Prepared for the owner-authorized production promotion on November 5, 2026.
This is a compatible feature release; all four public routes remain available.

## Changes

- Story maps use mapcn and show published business addresses when a precise
  address match is available. City fallbacks stay explicitly approximate, and
  all stories remain in the Story Browser list.
- Business pins include story and address links, hover/focus tooltips, and a
  label on the last selected pin. Touch gestures support two-finger embedded
  panning, pinch zoom, and one-finger movement in the expanded map.
- Home, About and Contact use the integrated sponsor-focused copy, dated view
  evidence, updated brand assets and self-hosted Manrope typography. Curated
  story titles and corrected city assignments are preserved separately from
  refreshed YouTube metadata.
- Partnership inquiries use the direct-send flow with verification, preserved
  entries on failure and retry feedback.
- The catalog refresh includes cached business geocoding. Local and CI checks
  cover map interactions, accessibility, fonts, content and production assets.

## Recovery and limits

The source and static asset changes are reversible through a previous Worker
deployment or a Git revert. No stored service data is migrated or deleted.
Published business addresses do not prove the filming location or current
business operation; ambiguous and unmatched addresses keep city fallbacks.
Physical phones, real screen readers and actual 400% zoom remain manual checks.
Contact browser tests mock verification and delivery; they do not establish
real inbox receipt.

Production is deployed by the existing main-branch GitHub Actions workflow.
The promotion PR records the exact tested source and check results; its merge
commit and the Deploy run identify the released source and artifact.
