# Business-owner testimonial interviews

Start with two or three owners who are willing to describe their experience. Record in the language they are most comfortable speaking; Arabic recordings can have approved English subtitles and a transcript on the English site.

## Before recording

- Confirm the business name, speaker's name and role, location, original Ya Hala feature URL, and publication date.
- Explain where the recording, name, business logo, and excerpts will appear. Obtain written permission and retain the original recording and approval.
- Invite an honest account. Do not give the owner a success claim to repeat.

## Interview prompts

1. What was happening in your business before the Ya Hala visit, and what were you hoping for?
2. What did Ya Hala feature about the business?
3. What did you notice after publication? Can you give a specific example?
4. Did customers say how they discovered you? How did you track that?
5. How long did you continue noticing the effect?
6. Were other promotions or changes happening at the same time?
7. If the business expanded, what part do you believe the feature played, and what supports that account?
8. What would you tell another business owner considering working with Ya Hala?

## Prepare each example for publication

Include the original feature, business and speaker identity, dates, an approved excerpt, accurate English subtitles/transcript, and permission to publish. Let the owner approve edits and translations in context before publication.

Separate content results (dated views or engagement) from business results (owner-reported customer visits, inquiries, or sales). Label recollections as the owner's account. Use measured figures only with supporting records and a defined period. Do not imply the feature alone caused an expansion, promise similar results, invent a quotation, or publish comparisons with other influencers from secondhand recollection.

No testimonial is ready for the site until the recording, translation where needed, supporting details, and publication approval are complete.
